----- EN_01.mp3 -----
We present the Interactive Syllabus for Decision Making and Strategic Communication in Health.
It was built by Daniel Rodríguez and Juan Pablo Arrobo.
It turns the course syllabus into a website that students can explore from their phones.

----- EN_02.mp3 -----
Our course is part of the Master's program in Health Management.
Until now the syllabus reached students only as a P D F file.
Everything was there, but in a long and static document that was hard to navigate.
The clearest problem appeared with the readings.
The reference articles were listed in the syllabus.
Even so students did not see how they connected to the development of the course.
A list of references does not explain when or why to read each article.

----- EN_03.mp3 -----
The users are the students of the course.
They receive a single link.
When they open it they find the schedule of the four sessions and the grading scheme.
They also see the resources, the bibliography and the instructors' contact details.
In addition they can keep simple track of their weekly tasks.
Everything is available in Spanish and in English.
The site is based on guide twenty one of our course.

----- EN_04.mp3 -----
The process has four steps.
First we started from the approved syllabus and the list of resources.
Then Claude generated the website from that material.
Each section comes from a specific part of the syllabus.
Next we as instructors reviewed every version against the syllabus and asked for corrections.
Claude never published anything on its own.
That is how we reached five versions.
Finally we published the site on GitHub Pages.
The A I proposes the content and the design.
People always decide what gets published, which dates are valid and which resources are included.

----- EN_05.mp3 -----
We verified four things.
The content and the dates of the four sessions match the approved syllabus.
In our last review we found a mismatch in the course period and corrected it.
The site works on four different phones.
Three out of three colleagues found the navigation simple.
Our review as instructors added the English version and more resources in each version.
We have not yet tested the site with our students.
For now that remains a proposal for the next term.

----- EN_06.mp3 -----
The site does not replace the live online classes.
It does not replace Moodle either.
Moodle remains the official channel for submitting assignments and taking exams.
The site does not ask for names or email addresses.
Task checkmarks stay only in each student's browser.
Our next step is to link the site from Moodle.
Then we will test it with students next term.

----- EN_07.mp3 -----
Let's look at the site.

On the right there is a one minute video that summarizes the course.

----- EN_08.mp3 -----
This is the reference articles section.
Here is the answer to our problem.
Each article is labeled with the week when it is used in class.
The audio summaries are recorded in Spanish and in English.
It also includes a short summary and an audio summary.

----- EN_09.mp3 -----
This is the weekly task list, called My tasks by week.
Readings and activities appear next to the date of each class.
